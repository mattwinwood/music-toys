Music Toys

Eight experiments. Are any of these fun?

Each one is its own thing. No connections, no funnel, no "now upgrade to the pro version." Just little machines for messing with music.

001

The Sound Of…

Type any noun, phrase, memory, or feeling. Get a real song that is that thing. Burnt toast. Tuesday afternoon in 1997. Leaving home at 19.

Play → 002

The Song You Almost Remember

Describe a song you can't quite place. Even just a mood, a TV show, a piece of a melody. The detective tries to find it.

Play → 003

Decade Compass

Drag a point on a 2D map — year on one axis, energy on the other. The song playing crossfades to whatever lives at that exact coordinate.

Play → 004

Two Truths and a Lie

Three songs. Two share a hidden trait — same producer, same drummer, same studio. One doesn't. Pick the lie.

Play → 005

Songs That Don't Exist

Describe a song that doesn't exist. "If My Chemical Romance had a folk album." "What Drake would sound like in 1968." Get the closest real thing.

Play → 006

Year Wars

Two songs play. You bet which is older. The genre fakes you out more often than you'd think. Streak counter, scoreboard.

Play → 007

The Album Nobody Made

Type a hypothetical album. "If Frank Ocean made a Christmas album." Get a 5-track tracklist of real songs, sequenced. Listen straight through.

Play → 008

Music Tarot

Tell me about your week. Three cards face down — Past, Present, Future. Tap to flip. Three songs reveal where you've been and where you're going.
